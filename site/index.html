01 RESEARCH

Listening to the brain.
Learning its language.

I’m interested in the neural dynamics that make communication possible—and in building decoders that can work with their variability.

My experience spans intracranial recordings in patients, noninvasive MEG experiments, and real-time decoding systems. Today, I focus on the timing of speech-related neural activity and the information it carries.

I

Neural timing & dynamics

Modeling trial-specific temporal variability in speech-related MEG, with learnable time warping to improve neural decoding.

MEGTemporal warping
II

Speech neuroprostheses

Decoding imagined and overt speech from human intracranial signals, and connecting neural representations to speech acoustics.

iEEG / ECoGSpeech decoding
III

Experiments to interfaces

Designing synchronized MEG/OPM speech paradigms and building pipelines that take neural signals from acquisition to online decoding.

OPM-MEGReal-time BCI
